Draft for review. This policy is written from how ProLift works today. Meet needs to confirm the highlighted details, and a legal adviser should check it before launch.

The short version
  • This website uses no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Your details are used only to review your profile and deliver the service you choose.
  • We never sell your details or share them for marketing.
  • Ask us any time for a copy of your details or to delete them.

01Who we are

ProLift Careers is a founder-led career studio run by Meet Aghara. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean ProLift Careers.

Registered name and address: ProLift Careers, a sole proprietorship of Meet Vipulkumar Aghara, Flat 103, Punyabhumi Apartment, behind Tata showroom, 150 Feet Ring Road, Rajkot, Gujarat 360007. Udyam (MSME) registration UDYAM-GJ-20-0308485.

02What we collect

Only what you choose to send us, through the website form, WhatsApp, LinkedIn or email:

  • your name, email address and WhatsApp number;
  • your target country, target role and LinkedIn profile link;
  • anything you write about your job search;
  • your resume and any other documents you share with us.

If you become a client, we also keep the job search information we work on together, such as the roles you approve and the applications in your tracker.

03How we use it

  • to review your resume and LinkedIn and reply with honest advice;
  • to deliver the service you choose, including writing your documents;
  • when you use our application service, to send your documents to employers and recruiters for roles you have approved;
  • to stay in touch with you about your search.

We don't sell your details and we don't share them with anyone for marketing.

04This website

The website uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. Its fonts and images are served from our own site, not from third parties.

The free profile review form doesn't store anything on our website. When you press send, it opens WhatsApp with your details filled in, and nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp.

Like any website, our hosting provider may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the site secure and running. Hosting provider: To confirm: hosting provider

05WhatsApp and LinkedIn

Messages you send us on WhatsApp are handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under its own privacy policy, and anything you do on LinkedIn is covered by LinkedIn's policy. We only see what you send us there.

06How long we keep it

We keep your details only as long as we need them for your review or service, and for any records the law requires. Retention period: To confirm: how long Meet keeps client files

07Your choices

You can ask us at any time for a copy of the details we hold about you, to correct them, or to delete them. We'll confirm once it's done.

08Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
