FAQ

Questions about
Profile Rebuild.

Which country formats do you write?

Indian, Gulf, UK, European, North American and Australian formats. The CV formats page shows what recruiters in each region expect.

Can I get only the resume, or only LinkedIn?

Profile Rebuild is one bundle: we rebuild both together, so your resume and your LinkedIn profile tell the same story to every recruiter who checks.

How many revisions do I get?

3 revision rounds on each: on the resume and on the LinkedIn profile.

Will recruiters message me?

We can't promise messages, and we won't. We position your profile so it shows up when recruiters search for your target role.
