FAQ

Questions about
Market Report.

What does the report cover?

Your profession in the countries you're targeting: demand for your role, the job titles and skills employers use, who is hiring, and how your profile compares. Message Meet for the full list of contents.

Who is it for?

Anyone planning a move to a new country, a career switch, or a search that isn't working, who wants a clear picture before applying.

Is it the same as a job search package?

No. The report tells you where to aim. Profile Rebuild and the Job Search Plans do the work of getting you there.

How much does it cost?

It's a one-time price, shown in your currency on the Pricing page.

Is this a promise of interviews?

No. It describes the market on the day it's written, from named sources.

How long does it take?

2 working days after we receive your details.

What do you need from me?

Your resume, the roles you're considering, the countries or cities, and your timeline.

What if I buy a plan afterwards?

The full ₹999 comes off Profile Rebuild or any plan started within 30 days of receiving the report.
