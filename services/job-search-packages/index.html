FAQ

Questions about
Job Search Plans.

Which plan should I choose?

Starter for entry-level and focused single-region searches. Growth for mid-career professionals, notice-period searches and career switchers. Global Premium for senior professionals, urgent searches and global relocation.

Do you apply manually?

Yes, every application is a real human decision. No auto-apply bots, no spam, no spray-and-pray. We apply to roles that actually match your level, geography and direction.

Why are your application numbers lower than auto-apply tools?

Because we don't apply to everything. Roles below a 70% match don't get your name. Between 70 and 80% we use your master resume; above 80% we rewrite it fully for that job. Fewer applications, far higher fit.

Do the packages include a new resume and LinkedIn profile?

Not a full rebuild: that's Profile Rebuild, which you can add to any package. Inside the packages, Starter includes an ATS review of your resume, and Growth and Global Premium rewrite it for each of your 80%+ matches.

What if I'm on a notice period?

This is exactly what the plans are built for. You focus on your current job. We manage the structured search in the background: applications, outreach, tracking, weekly review.
