The process

Four weeks. One operating system
for your job search.

Week 01Audit

Diagnose, don't decorate

Resume, LinkedIn, target roles, target country and region, current pipeline. We map what's broken and what's wasting time.

Week 01–02Build

Position with precision

ATS resume rewritten in your region's format. LinkedIn rebuilt for recruiter search. Voice and positioning locked in.

Week 02–04Apply

Apply with strategy

JD-based CVs for priority roles. Manual applications to real matches. Recruiter outreach starts in parallel.

OngoingTrack

Track what's working

One tracker. Weekly review. Adjust the strategy as the data comes in. No applying into the void.

The honest part

What we won't do.
Read this before you pay.

×

No job guarantees.

We don't guarantee jobs, interviews, offers or selection. Those depend on company requirements, competition, salary expectations and interview performance.

×

Fewer applications than bots.

Roles below a 70% match don't get your name. If you want hundreds of generic applications a week, we're not the right fit.

×

English-language roles only.

Language-specific applications, like a German-language Lebenslauf and cover letter, aren't offered yet. If that affects your target roles, we'll tell you upfront.

×

We need you in the loop.

Your resume, LinkedIn, target roles, country and notice period. Without them, nobody can give you an honest answer, including us.
