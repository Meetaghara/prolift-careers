FAQ

Questions about
pricing.

Which currency do I pay in?

Prices show in INR, USD, GBP, EUR, AED, CAD and AUD. If your currency isn't listed, prices are in USD.

Is there a long contract?

No. Each plan runs one cycle, which ends when your applications are delivered, usually 30 to 45 days. After each cycle you decide whether to continue.

Do the plans include a new resume and LinkedIn profile?

Growth and Global Premium do: your resume is fully rebuilt, your LinkedIn optimized, and resumes rewritten for your best-matched jobs. Starter checks your resume and sets it in your region's format, but doesn't rebuild it.

Can I buy the portfolio website on its own?

Yes. It's included in the Growth and Global Premium packages, and you can also buy it on its own as a one-time service.

Do you guarantee a job or interviews?

No, and nobody honestly can. We guarantee the work: a clearer profile, sharper positioning, applications only to roles that match, and a search you can track. Hiring decisions stay with the employer.

Not sure where to start?

Send your resume and the roles you want. I'll tell you which service fits, and which doesn't.
