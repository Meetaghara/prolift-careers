The short version
  • We improve your resume, LinkedIn, applications, outreach and tracking.
  • We don't guarantee jobs, interviews, offers or selection.
  • We only apply to roles you approve, and we never invent experience.
  • You give us accurate information and timely feedback.

01Our services

ProLift Careers offers ATS resume writing, LinkedIn optimization, JD-based CVs, recruiter mapping and outreach, manual job applications and application tracking. What each package includes is agreed with you before you pay.

02No job guarantees

We guarantee a clearer profile, sharper positioning and a job search process you can manage. We don't guarantee jobs, interviews, offers or selection. Those depend on company requirements, competition, salary expectations and interview performance.

03What we need from you

  • accurate information about your experience, education and right to work;
  • your current resume, LinkedIn, target roles, target country and notice period;
  • timely feedback on drafts and on the roles we suggest.

04Honest documents

We rewrite and position your real experience. We don't invent experience, skills or qualifications, and we don't use keyword stuffing or hidden text.

05Applications on your behalf

On application plans, we apply only to roles that match your target and that you have approved. Roles below a 70% match are skipped. Interviews, offers and decisions remain yours.

06Revisions and timelines

Resume, LinkedIn Profile and Profile Rebuild include 3 revision rounds. Portfolio Website includes 2 revision rounds before handover, and the project closes once you approve the site.

Turnaround, in working days after we receive your details: Resume 2, LinkedIn Profile 2, Profile Rebuild 4 to 5, Market Report 2, Portfolio Website 6.
