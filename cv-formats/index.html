FAQ

Indian resume questions

Do Indian recruiters need a photo?

Not for most private-sector roles. Recruiters judge the resume on skills and results, and a photo takes space a summary could use. Some government application forms ask for one; follow the form when it does.

Should I mention my notice period?

Yes. Indian recruiters ask about it on the first call, so a line like 'Notice period: 60 days (negotiable)' near your contact details saves a round of back-and-forth.

How long should an Indian resume be?

One page for freshers, two pages once you have a few years of experience. Past two pages, recruiters tend to skim.

Is an objective statement still needed?

No. Replace it with a 3 to 4 line profile summary: your role, years of experience and the strengths that match the job.

FAQ

Gulf CV questions

Do Gulf recruiters expect a photo?

Often, yes. A small, professional, passport-style photo is common on CVs for the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Qatar. Use a plain background and business clothing.

Should I list nationality and visa status?

Yes. Gulf recruiters filter on these early. Put your nationality in your personal details and state your visa status clearly near the top, for example 'UAE residence visa, transferable' rather than 'Visa: yes'.

UAE vs Saudi: any difference?

The format is much the same. The differences are in the details: Saudi employers work under Saudization quotas, so some roles are open only to Saudi nationals, and Arabic is asked for more often. We adjust the CV to the country you're targeting.

Should I mention expected salary?

Not on the CV. Share it when a recruiter asks, usually on the first call or in the application form, and say whether your figure includes allowances such as housing and transport.

FAQ

UK CV questions

Does a UK CV need a photo?

No. UK employers don't expect one, and many prefer CVs without photos to keep hiring fair. Leave out your date of birth and marital status for the same reason.

Should I use UK spelling?

Yes. Use UK English throughout: 'organised', 'optimised', 'programme'. US spelling tells a UK recruiter the CV wasn't written for them.

What's the right length for a UK CV?

Two pages of A4 for most experienced professionals. One page is fine for graduates.

Do I need references on my CV?

No. Employers ask for referees at the offer stage. Skip the 'references available on request' line and use the space for results.

FAQ

Europe CV questions

Is a photo expected in Europe?

It depends on the country. In Germany and Austria a professional photo is still common, though it varies by industry. Employers in the Netherlands and Ireland usually don't expect one. We follow the norm of the country you're applying to.

Should I use the Europass format?

Use it as a starting point, not as your final CV. Europass is widely known, but many private employers prefer a CV designed around the job. Germany's official Make it in Germany portal gives the same advice.

Do you handle German-language roles?

Not yet. We write CVs for English-language roles across Europe. A German-language Lebenslauf and cover letter aren't offered at the moment, and we'll tell you upfront if that affects your target roles.

How do I show language levels?

Use the CEFR scale, from A1 (beginner) to C2 (mastery), for example 'German B2'. European recruiters know the scale, and it's clearer than 'good' or 'fluent'.

FAQ

US & Canada resume questions

Are photos allowed on US resumes?

Leave the photo out. US and Canadian employers avoid photos to keep hiring free of bias, and a resume with one can count against you.

One page or two?

In the US, one page if you have under about 10 years of experience; two pages are fine for senior roles where the second page carries real results. In Canada, 2 pages is normal, and 3 for senior roles.

How is a Canadian resume different?

Very little. Use Canadian spelling ('centre', 'colour'), mention French if you have it (it matters in Quebec and for many federal jobs), and state your work permit or PR status.

Do I list references?

No. Give them when the employer asks, usually after the final interview.

FAQ

Australian resume questions

Do Australian employers want a photo?

No. Leave out your photo, age and marital status. Australian employers judge the resume on experience and fit.

How long should my resume be?

Two pages is enough for internships and graduate roles. Experienced professionals can go to three or four pages, as long as every page adds real detail.

Should I list referees?

Yes, it's common. Most employers want at least two professional referees. You can name them or write 'Referees available on request'.

What are selection criteria?

Some roles, especially in government, ask you to address key selection criteria: a short written response showing how you meet each listed requirement. Treat it as part of the application, not an extra.
