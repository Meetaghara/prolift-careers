About ProLift

A small studio that runs your search like a process.

Founder-led, based in Rajkot, working with clients wherever the job is.

Why ProLift exists

I trained as an organic chemist, with an MSc from GSFC University in Vadodara. Then I spent years in technical sales and site troubleshooting in my family's construction chemicals business in Rajkot, and co-founded an agri-export venture. In 2025 I was one of 525 Yatris picked for Jagriti Yatra, out of more than 70,000 applicants.

None of that sounds like careers work. All of it shaped how ProLift runs. In a lab, a reaction fails for a reason: the wrong input, the wrong conditions, the wrong measurement. A job search fails the same way. When 80 applications turn into two calls, the person usually isn't the problem. The inputs are.

So we treat a search as a process. Pick the right roles. Build the resume and LinkedIn around them. Apply only where the match is real. Then do the part almost nobody does: find the actual person behind each role and write to them, in your voice, for you to send from your own account.

ProLift is small on purpose. It's me and one delivery associate. I quality-check every resume, write the outreach on Growth myself, and run every Global Premium search end to end. That's why I can read every profile myself.

What I won't do: promise you a job, ask for your passwords, or apply to roles that don't fit. What I will do: show you every application, every message and every number, every week.

What we're not

ProLift is a career consultancy, not a recruiter. We don't place anyone with an employer and we don't take a cut of anyone's salary. You pay us to run your search: positioning, applications, and mapping the people behind the roles and writing the messages, which you send from your own account.
